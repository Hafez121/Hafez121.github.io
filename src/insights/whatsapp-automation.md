For many businesses, WhatsApp is the front desk. Customers ask about opening hours, prices, delivery, availability and directions, often the same questions, often late at night.

Answering every one by hand eats hours. Not answering loses customers. Automation sits in between, but only if you are clear about which messages a machine should handle and which ones need a person.

## What the free WhatsApp Business app already does

Before paying for anything, check what the WhatsApp Business app gives you for free:

- **A business profile** with your description, address, hours and category.
- **Greeting messages** for people who write to you for the first time.
- **Away messages** that reply automatically outside your working hours.
- **Quick replies:** saved answers you send with a shortcut instead of typing them again.
- **Labels** to sort chats, for example new customer, waiting for payment, done.
- **A catalog** so customers can browse products or services inside the chat.

Set these up well and a small business can cut a large share of repetitive typing without any custom work.

## When the free app stops being enough

The app is built for one business owner on one phone. Its automation is basic: greetings, away messages and saved replies. It does not run full chatbots, connect to your other tools, or give a team a shared inbox.

When you need those things, the next step is the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), usually through a provider. That is where real flows become possible: booking a slot, checking an order status, sending a confirmation, passing the chat to the right person.

## Automate these

- Opening hours, location and directions.
- Delivery areas, how ordering works, payment methods.
- Confirmations and reminders for bookings or orders.
- The first reply after hours, so nobody feels ignored.

## Keep these human

- Complaints and anything where the customer is upset.
- Custom quotes and negotiations.
- Sensitive or personal information.
- Anything where a wrong answer costs more than a slow one.

A good automated setup always tells the customer how to reach a person, and makes that handoff easy. If people feel trapped talking to a machine, the automation has saved you time by losing you customers.

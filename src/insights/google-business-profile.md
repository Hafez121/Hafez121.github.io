Search for a pharmacy, a mechanic or a coffee shop on your phone and look at what appears first. Usually it is not a website. It is a map with a few business listings under it, each with hours, photos, reviews, a call button and a directions button.

For a lot of people, that listing is the whole decision. They call, or they tap directions, and they never visit the website at all. That listing is your Google Business Profile, and it deserves the same care as your website.

## How Google decides who shows up

Google's own help page names three things that decide local results:

- **Relevance:** how well your profile matches what the person searched for.
- **Distance:** how far you are from the person searching, or from the place they searched.
- **Prominence:** how well known your business is, including links, reviews and ratings.

You cannot change your distance to a customer. You can change the other two, and most businesses leave easy points on the table.

## What to get right

**Pick the right primary category.** It is one of the strongest relevance signals you control. Be specific: "Italian restaurant" beats "Restaurant".

**Keep hours accurate, including holidays.** Nothing loses trust faster than showing up to a closed door that the map said was open.

**Fill in everything.** Services, description, attributes, website, phone. An empty field is a question the customer has to ask someone else.

**Add real photos, and keep adding them.** Your front door, inside, your team, your work. People want to know what they will find when they arrive.

**Answer reviews, all of them.** A short, calm reply to a bad review often does more for the next customer than the review itself does damage.

**Match your details everywhere.** Your name, address and phone should read the same on your profile, your website and anywhere else you are listed.

## Where your website fits

The profile gets people to choose you. The website is where they go when they want more: menus, price lists, portfolios, booking. A good setup links the two both ways, so each one makes the other more credible.

If your profile is unclaimed, out of date or empty, fix that before spending money on anything else online. It is usually the fastest win available.

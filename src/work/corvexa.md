## The brief

A wholesale distributor needs a site that explains what it supplies, shows where it operates, and lets new retail partners apply without phone calls and email back-and-forth.

## What I built

- **A custom WordPress theme written from scratch.** No page builder, so the markup stays lean and every part of the layout is under control.
- **Custom post types** for the content the business manages often, so editing stays simple in the admin.
- **An interactive locations map** built with Leaflet.js.
- **A partner registration form** that submits over AJAX, validates on the server and is protected by reCAPTCHA.
- **An animated brand-partner marquee** on the homepage.

## Why it is built this way

A distributor's site is mostly structured content: product categories, locations, partner brands. Custom post types fit that shape better than a page builder does, and they keep the admin tidy for whoever runs the site later.

## The brief

A restaurant whose guests speak Arabic and English needs one page that works properly in both languages, loads fast, and helps search engines understand what the place is and where it is.

## What I built

- **A full English and Arabic toggle.** Switching language flips the whole layout to right-to-left and swaps the fonts, instead of only translating the words.
- **Scroll-reveal animations** and an **animated sourcing map** that shows where ingredients come from.
- **JSON-LD structured data** describing the restaurant, so search engines can read its details directly.
- **No frameworks and no build step.** Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript keep the page small and fast.

## Why it matters

Arabic support is often added as an afterthought: text is translated but the layout still reads left to right. Building RTL in from the start avoids the broken alignment and mirrored icons that make bilingual sites feel unfinished.

## The brief

An online clothing store that lets shoppers browse by collection and brand, read product details and reviews, and check out without friction.

## What I built

- **Product listings** with filtering by brand.
- **Collections and new-arrivals sections** on the homepage.
- **Single product pages** with customer reviews.
- **The full WooCommerce flow** from cart to checkout.
